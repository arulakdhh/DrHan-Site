Our Story

It started with a simple idea.

In 2021, a small team in Chennai asked one question: why should a hospital ever have to wait for the device it needs?

We started by supplying Apollo Hospitals and Fortis Malar. Working with them taught us what busy hospitals really need: quick answers, honest stock updates, and deliveries that arrive when we say they will.

We took that lesson with us, from Tambaram to Vellore to Chidambaram. Today we work with hospitals, clinics and labs across Tamil Nadu and Pondicherry, and we're just getting started.

— The Dr. Han Healthcare team

Which areas do you serve?

Chennai and its suburbs, Vellore, Villupuram, Cuddalore, Panruti, Vadalur, Chidambaram and Pondicherry. We're expanding across India, so ask us even if your town isn't listed.

What products can you supply?

Through our partnership with Meril, we cover 17 specialty areas, including cardiovascular, orthopaedics, trauma, endo-surgery, diagnostics, ENT and critical care. Tell us what you need and we'll check for you.

Do you work with small clinics, or only big hospitals?

Both. We supply large multi-specialty hospitals, neighbourhood clinics, diagnostic labs and diagnostic centres. Every customer gets the same care.

How do I place an order?

The easiest way is to call or message us, or use the form below. We'll confirm availability and pricing with you before anything is dispatched.

I'm a device manufacturer. Can we partner with you?

Yes. We're open to working with Indian and international manufacturers. Choose "Supply partner" in the form below and our team will reach out.
